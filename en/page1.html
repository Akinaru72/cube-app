Building the Cross

The cube has a right, left, top, and bottom face. While performing the algorithms, do not change the cube’s orientation. Always keep the cube in the same position relative to yourself.

The center pieces always form fixed opposite pairs. Therefore, the white center is always opposite the yellow center, the red center is opposite the orange center, and the blue center is opposite the green center.

Cube notation is written mainly with Latin letters. Each letter represents a clockwise turn of a specific face. If a letter is followed by an apostrophe ('), the face must be turned counterclockwise. A number indicates how many turns should be made. For example, the notation R2 means turning the right face twice (180°).

Choose which colors will be on the top and bottom of your cube and remember them. For example, we will use blue on the bottom and green on the top. Keep the cube in this orientation until the first layer is completely solved.
