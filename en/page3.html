Solving the Middle Layer

Before starting to solve the second layer, hold the cube with the completed side facing down. In our case, this is the blue face.

Find any edge piece on the top layer that does not contain the green color. In our case, this is the color of the center on the face opposite the blue one.

Turn the top face until the side color of the required edge matches the center of the corresponding side face. Below are three possible cases and their solutions.

U R U' R'
F R' F' R
U' F' U F
R' F R F'

Third Case

The third case occurs when the edge piece is already in its correct position, but is flipped by 180°.

In this situation, the required edge must be "knocked out" using any edge that contains the green color.

Choose either of the two algorithms shown above. After that, the required edge will return to the top layer.

Then repeat the same procedure once again, and the edge will move into its correct position.
