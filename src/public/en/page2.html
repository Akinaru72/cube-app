Solving the Corners

After completing the cross, we move on to solving the corners of the first layer.

Each corner consists of three colors. In our case, one of them must be blue, while the other two must match the colors of the side faces. We need to place the blue-yellow-red corner between the corresponding center pieces. Find the required corner and rotate the bottom face so that the corner is positioned directly underneath the place where it belongs. The illustration below shows three possible positions.

The first position can be transformed into the other two using the algorithms R F' R' F or F' R F R'.

After placing one corner, move on to the next one and repeat the same procedure.

Important

If you perform the algorithm F' R F R' or R F' R' F six times in a row, the cube will return to its original state. Any of the three corner positions can be solved using one of these two algorithms.

Conclusion

Four repetitions of F' R F R' are equivalent to two repetitions of R F' R' F. Likewise, four repetitions of R F' R' F are equivalent to two repetitions of F' R F R'.

Therefore, it is enough to memorize just one of these algorithms — that will be completely sufficient.
