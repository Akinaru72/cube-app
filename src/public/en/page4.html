Edge Orientation

Rotate the top face and check how many edge pieces match the centers of the side faces. Ideally, all four colors should match. If they do not, find a position where two adjacent edges match.

If only two opposite edges match, or if none of them match, perform the algorithm R U R' U R U2 R' U, then check the edge positions again.

If only two adjacent edges match, hold the cube so that these edges are on the BACK and RIGHT faces. Then perform the algorithm shown below.

R U R' U R U2 R' U
